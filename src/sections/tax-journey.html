SCIENTIFIC PAPER · EMPIRICAL TAXATION STUDY

The Role of Digital Transformation in Mitigating Tax Aggressiveness in Energy and Industrial Companies

An empirical study of how technology adoption and digital transformation disclosure can mitigate aggressive tax avoidance among energy and industrial companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange from 2021 to 2025.

✓
Empirical Evidence: Shows that transparent corporate digital governance significantly reduces tax aggressiveness and supports tax compliance.
✓
Robust Methodology: Panel data regression using the Random Effects Model (REM), controlling for leverage, profitability, and capital intensity.
